ARTEMIS

2024. The Goddess of the Hunt.

The robot

Artemis was our robot for the FIRST Challenge CRESCENDO. A quick, light robot with a pivoting shooter and swerve drive, it was an Event Finalist at Auburn and a Semi-Finalist at Sammamish.

CRESCENDO

Alliances score points by launching orange rings called “Notes” into the Speaker or placing them into the Amp. When Notes are placed in the Amp, further Notes scored in the Speaker are “Amplified” and worth more points. In the Endgame, robots try to elevate themselves on one of three chains hanging from the Stage near center field.

Performance

Auburn Finalist

Defeating the 1st Alliance in her first match, Artemis and her Alliance 8 partners, 1983 Skunk Works and 9023 Future Martians, made it to the finals of the Auburn District Event.

Creativity Award

Awarded for our custom 3D-printed bumper spacers, which absorb impacts and expand Artemis’ perimeter without changing the drivetrain size.

Sammamish Semi-Finalist

Ranked 15th in qualification matches and picked by the 3rd Alliance to join 9036 Ramen Robotics and 9442 Miso Mechanics, Artemis made it to the semi-finals of the Sammamish District Event.

Skyline Spartabots
