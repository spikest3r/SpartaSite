History

Every season since 2011, from our first regionals to the 2018 World Championship.

2026

HADES

Hades is the Spartabots’ 2026 robot. Watch the season showcase on the home page.

2024

ARTEMIS

FIRST CRESCENDO

Artemis was our robot for the FIRST Challenge CRESCENDO. It was an Event Finalist at Auburn and a Semi-Finalist at Sammamish. Season details

2023

ZEUS

FIRST CHARGED UP

Zeus was our robot for CHARGED UP. It was part of Alliance 5 at Sammamish and Alliance 6 at Glacier Peak. Season details

2022

CHRONOS

FIRST RAPID REACT

Chronos was our 2022 competition robot for RAPID REACT. It made it to Worlds in the Hopper Division as a Quarter-Finalist. Season details

2020

OLYMPUS

FIRST INFINITE RECHARGE

Olympus was our 2020 robot for INFINITE RECHARGE. We were Glacier Peak finalists with a qualifying record of 11-7-0.

2019

KHAOS

FIRST DEEP SPACE

Khaos was our 2019 robot for DEEP SPACE. We were Hopper Division finalists with a qualifying record of 39-29-0.

2018

HERMES

FIRST POWER UP

Hermes was our 2018 robot for POWER UP. We were the World Champions with a qualifying record of 41-30-0. This was our first year with robot names.

2017

STEAMWORKS

We competed in STEAMWORKS at Auburn Mountainview and Glacier Peak. Our qualifying record was 12-12-2.

2016

STRONGHOLD

In STRONGHOLD, the Spartabots won the Glacier Peak event with a qualifying record of 12-13-0.

2015

RECYCLE RUSH

We competed in RECYCLE RUSH at Auburn Mountainview and the Mount Vernon event. Our qualifying score was 57.83 and our playoff score was 63.25.

2014

ARIEL ASSIST

In 2014 the Spartabots were finalists at Glacier Peak and took part in the District Championship, with a qualifying record of 22-21-1.

2013

ULTIMATE ASCENT

We competed in ULTIMATE ASCENT at the Central Washington Regional and won the Creativity Award sponsored by Xerox. Our qualifying record was 7-4-0.

2012

REBOUND RUMBLE

We competed in REBOUND RUMBLE at the Seattle Cascade Regional. Our qualifying record was 3-8-0.

2011

LOGO MOTION

We competed in LOGO MOTION at the Seattle Olympic Regional. Our qualifying record was 4-4-2.

Skyline Spartabots
