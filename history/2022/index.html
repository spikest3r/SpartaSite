CHRONOS

2022. The Personification of Time.

The robot

Equipped with a stowable intake and a Limelight vision system, Chronos was our 2022 robot. Chronos was a Quarter-Finalist in the Hopper Division and a Finalist at the Auburn, Bonney Lake and Pacific Northwest District Events.

RAPID REACT

Alliances score points by launching balls into the Lower and Upper Hubs. Robots collect game pieces from Terminals on either side of the field. In the Endgame, robots climb a set of bars in the Hangar to earn additional points.

Performance

Hopper Division Quarter-Finalist

Chronos was the 3rd pick of Alliance 5 in the Hopper Division. The alliance made it to the quarter-finals, but lost to the 4th Alliance.

Pacific Northwest District Finalist

Chronos was the Captain of Alliance 2, selecting 488 XBOT and 2046 Bear Metal as partners. The alliance made it to the finals, but lost to the 1st Alliance.
