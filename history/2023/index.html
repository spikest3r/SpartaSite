ZEUS

2023. The God of the Sky.

The robot

Zeus was our 2023 robot. A tall, mighty robot with an elevator to raise its arm and a swerve drive, Zeus was selected for the 5th and 6th alliances at the Sammamish and Glacier Peak events, respectively.

CHARGED UP

Alliances score points by placing cubes and cones onto platforms and poles in front of the driver station walls. Groups of 3 game pieces form Links, and enough Links earn the Alliance the Sustainability Bonus. In the Endgame, robots try to balance on the Charge Station.

Performance

Sammamish Alliance 5

Zeus was ranked 8th and was the first pick of Alliance 5. Zeus competed alongside 1899 Saints Robotics and 9023 Future Martians, reaching the 3rd round of the playoffs.

Glacier Peak Alliance 6

The first choice of Alliance 6 at Glacier Peak, Zeus teamed up with 4681 Murphy’s Law and 3049 BremerTron and made it 2 rounds into the playoffs.
