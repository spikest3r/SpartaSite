The Spartabots

About us

A completely student-run team that builds a competition robot in six weeks.

Club overview

We are the Spartabots, a robotics club participating in the FIRST Robotics Competition as Team 2976. In six weeks, our completely student-run team uses problem solving, critical thinking and analytical skills to create a robot.

The club was created in 2008 and initially worked in the garage of one of our mentors. In the first few years we struggled, but always managed to bring a robot to competitions. During that time our team never advanced to the elimination rounds of any competition, but by 2014 the Spartabots had made it through two district competitions, getting second place in one of them, and qualified for regionals.

Since then, we’ve won the World Championship (2018) and qualified for Worlds 4 times, making us one of the most successful teams in the Pacific Northwest.

Our mission

Our mission is to inspire students in the fields of science, technology, engineering and math. We help students cultivate these skills by being part of a collaborative team that learns and grows together.

Community

One of our goals is to raise awareness and interest in our club, and in science and technology, among the people in our community. Partnering with community members and local companies allows us to raise the funds to support our mission and objectives.

During the school year, our team participates in local elementary school science fairs where younger students can learn about robotics. Community festivals such as Salmon Days give us a venue to showcase our club. In the summer we staff a booth at the Issaquah Farmers Market, where the community can see our robots first-hand, and we take part in local parades and community projects.

To grow our club, we also do outreach within our school: a presence on the school website and our Facebook page, morning assemblies where we showcase our robot, posters and flyers around campus, and a booth at freshman orientation night so incoming students can preview the club.

We are also planning an “Awareness Night” to bring together and thank our school, sponsors and dedicated mentors for their support. Without them our team would not exist, and we would like to show our appreciation with an evening presenting what their contributions helped us achieve.

Sponsors

Skyline Robotics couldn’t exist without the support of our sponsors, who help in a variety of ways, from granting money to donating parts.

If you’re interested in sponsoring our club, please send us an email, or see our sponsors page.
